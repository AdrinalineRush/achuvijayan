I’m Achu Vijayan.
A filmmaker and editor shaping stories through image, rhythm and emotion.

My journey began in the editing room, finding the moments that make a story breathe. From the sets of Second Show to my directorial debut Vichithram, that instinct has stayed with me.

Today, my practice moves between narrative cinema, post-production and the possibilities of AI filmmaking. Based in Kerala. Creating beyond borders.
