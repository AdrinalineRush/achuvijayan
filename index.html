05 — BACKGROUND

Editorial Philosophy

I began my filmmaking journey on film sets in 2012, working as a spot editor on landmark releases like Second Show. Over twelve years in the editing suite cutting features for veterans like Mammootty (White) and high-voltage commercial hits (Oru Adaar Love, Charles Enterprises), I developed an intuitive instinct for timing, narrative breathing room, and visual tone.

In 2022, I made my feature directorial debut with Vichithram under Joy Movie Productions, crafting a mystery-thriller defined by atmospheric obsidian pacing and meticulous camera staging.

"An editor’s job isn't to cut film; it’s to preserve the emotional truth of an actor's performance and design the invisible rhythm of the story."

As designated partner at Joy Movie Productions, I co-produced Anand Ekarshi's chamber drama Aattam (2024), which received three National Film Awards including Best Feature Film of India, Best Screenplay, and Best Editing.
